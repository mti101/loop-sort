Loop Sort - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026 · Publisher: Terafort ("we", "us")

Loop Sort is a single-player puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles. We keep it as small as possible: there are no accounts, no sign-in, and we do not run our own servers that receive your personal data.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, boosters, lives, settings and daily-reward status) is stored locally on your device using Android's standard app storage. We do not receive this data. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads). To serve and measure ads, Google and its ad partners may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and similar device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language);
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data (impressions, clicks, crashes, performance).

This data is collected by Google, not by us, under Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

3. Consent (EEA, UK and other regions)

Where required by law, the app shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) on first launch so you can choose whether personalised ads may be used. You can change your choice any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app (shown where applicable).

4. In-app purchases

If you buy "Remove ads", the purchase is processed by Google Play. We receive only the confirmation that the purchase succeeded; we never see your payment details.

5. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location, microphone or camera data. We do not sell your personal data.

6. Children

Loop Sort is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to a maximum content rating of PG.

7. Data retention and your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to delete or export. Local game data can be erased by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. For data held by Google, please use Google's own privacy controls. Depending on where you live (for example GDPR/UK GDPR, CCPA), you may have rights regarding data processed by Google; see the Google policy linked above.

8. Security

All network traffic from the app (ads and purchases) is performed by Google's SDKs over encrypted connections.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the effective date above.
